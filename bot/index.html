04 · FAQ

The questions people actually ask.

Is there a free trial?+
No. The results are posted publicly every single day, win or lose, so you can watch it for as long as you like before deciding.
What do I need to run it?+
Three things: a paid TradingView plan, a broker or prop account that allows automated trading like Lucid Trading, and a TradersPost account. Both are billed by them, not by Levl. The full setup guide is public, so you can see exactly what you are getting into before you pay.
Do I need to be at the screen?+
No. TradingView fires the alert and TradersPost forwards it, so your computer can be off.
Can I run it on a prop account?+
That is what I run it on. Check your firm's rules on automated trading first, since some allow it and some do not. Firms running on ProjectX do not work.
What if it stops working?+
Then you cancel, and I will say so publicly rather than quietly. There is no guarantee of future results, and with a mechanical strategy there is a real chance of market phases where it underperforms. That is the trade you are making. I monitor it constantly and keep developing new strategies, and every update is included while you are subscribed.
Is this financial advice?+
No. It is software that places orders by fixed rules in your own account, at a risk per trade you choose, and you can switch it off at any moment. I never see or touch your money. Trading futures carries a substantial risk of loss. Full terms in the Risk & Disclosure.
