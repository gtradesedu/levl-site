Levl Up

Everything you look at on a chart, in one tool. Sweeps, closures, CISD, SMT, FVG, key levels. This guide walks every setting in panel order, so you can build the chart that fits how you trade.

Start on the defaults. Turn features on one at a time as you get comfortable.

01

GeneralGlobal display settings for the whole indicator.

Label Size
Size of every text label. Tiny Small Normal Large. Keep it small on busy charts.
Timezone
The display timezone for the candle time labels and the alert session windows (Asia / London / NY). Range UTC-12 to UTC+14. The core day/week logic and the fixed session lines (9:30, 18:00) are always anchored to New York and do not move with this setting. Set it to match how you read the chart, so labels and alert windows line up.
Bias
The master directional filter. Controls which formations show. Neutral both directions, Bullish only bull, Bearish only bear, Auto the indicator sets bias from daily structure and updates live when price breaks a key level or closes through equilibrium. Learn on Neutral, cut noise with a direction or Auto.
Show Bias Display
On-chart readout of the current bias. Most useful in Auto.
ADR
Average Daily Range over 20 days, plus how much price has used today. Controls: position, show, color. Context for whether a move is already extended.
Good to knowThe Bias readout and the ADR readout share one display block, so they use the same position and text color.
02

Closure FormationThe heart of the indicator. Controls the formations and how they align across timeframes.

History
How many past formations to keep per type. Low for live trading, high to study history.
Backtest
On keeps invalidated formations on the chart so you can study how they played out. Off for live.
TF Alignment
How the higher timeframe is chosen. There are always two timeframes: your chart and its HTF. Auto uses standard Fractal-Model alignment. Custom lets you set it yourself (field 1 = the chart TF the mapping applies to, field 2 = the HTF to use).
Closure Formation
The main break line at the closure level. Color, on/off, style, width. This one toggle controls the whole standard formation — break line, CISD, continuation and projection all hide together, because they are a single object.
CISD
Change in State of Delivery line. The confirmation trigger that makes a formation valid.
Pending CISD
The next potential CISD level, and where it would form.
Continuation CISD
After a valid closure, price should continue and form a continuation CISD. That is an entry opportunity.
SMT CISD
A CISD that confirms an SMT divergence. Combines the trigger with divergence for a higher-confluence entry.
CTF Closure Formation
Candle closure on the chart timeframe itself, for trading directly on a higher TF. Own color and toggle. Only on 15m charts and above.
Draw only at Key Levels
Show a closure formation only when it forms at a key level. Everything else stays hidden. The whole formation hides as one unit. Hidden formations are not deleted, turn the toggle off and they return. SMT formations always show. Full logic in section 5.
03

AdditionalSupporting levels.

PDH / PDL
Previous Day High and Low lines.
PWH / PWL
Previous Week High and Low.
EQ (Equilibrium)
The 50% midpoint of the relevant range. Both HTF Only Chart Only Latest only.
HTF Swings
Significant swing highs and lows on the HTF, with a Max count.
HTF² Swings
Swing highs and lows one level higher: the HTF of your HTF. On a 5m chart your HTF is 1H, so HTF² is Daily. On a 1H chart HTF is Daily, so HTF² is Monthly. These are the levels the big moves react to, now visible without leaving your chart. Color, style, Max. Default off. With TF Alignment on Custom, HTF² follows your custom mapping.
CTF Swings
The same, on your chart timeframe. All swing types show one bull and one bear line even at Max = 1.
Separator
Vertical lines where each HTF period begins. Has a Count.
Session Lines · 09:30 / 18:00
Opening price at two fixed New York times: 9:30 (NY stock open) and 18:00 (the day's open; forex charts use 17:00). Own color, style and toggle each. Intraday only.
Custom Session Line
Same mechanic, your chosen time. Enter any time as HH:MM in New York time and the indicator marks the opening price of that minute every day. Invalid input falls back to 10:30. Intraday only, on higher timeframes where no candle starts at that exact minute no line appears.
Fib Projection
Fibonacci target levels projected ahead from a valid formation's CISD range, giving objective take-profit zones. Separate Label checkbox.
T-Spot / EQ Fill
Highlights a reaction zone inside the formation. Bull and bear color, on/off. Only after a valid C2 closure (in C3 and C4) or a valid C3 closure (in C4).
04

FVGFair value gaps, across three timeframe levels.

CTF FVG
Fair value gaps on the chart timeframe. Bull and bear color, toggle, Max count, invalidation mode.
HTF FVG
The same, on the HTF.
HTF² FVG
Gaps two levels up (the HTF of your HTF, see HTF² Swings). A Daily gap on your 5m chart is a bigger magnet than a 1H one, and this draws it right on your chart. Same controls as HTF FVG: colors, toggle, Max, invalidation mode Close Through 50% Close. Default off.
iFVG (Inverse FVG)
When a regular FVG is fully traded through it can invert (support and resistance flip). A Max TF dropdown limits where it appears.
Bias Filter
Hides FVGs pointing against the current bias. Shows one bull and one bear even at Max = 1, but with the filter on you only see the one matching your direction.
Good to knowMax controls what is drawn, not what is remembered. Internally the indicator keeps up to 3× your Max per direction, and the Key Level Filter checks against that full memory. See section 5.
05

Key Level FilterThe full logic. This is the one worth reading twice.

Not every closure formation is worth your attention. The ones that form at a key level are. The filter marks every formation that forms at one, and lets you hide or mute the rest. Two independent toggles use that mark:

  • Draw only at Key Levels (Closure Formation group) — only key-level formations get drawn.
  • Key Level Filter (Alarm Filter group) — only key-level formations fire alerts.

They do not affect each other. You can filter alerts while still drawing everything, clean the chart while keeping all alerts, or both.

What counts as a key level

  • HTF swing highs and lows, and HTF² swing highs and lows
  • PDH / PDL and PWH / PWL
  • HTF FVGs and HTF² FVGs

What deliberately does not count: CTF swings, CTF FVGs, iFVGs, EQ, session lines. Chart-timeframe levels sit too close to price. If they counted, almost every formation would qualify and the filter would filter nothing.

How a formation earns the mark

When price breaks or trades into a key level, a 3-HTF-candle window opens. A formation only counts as at a key level if it confirms inside that window. Outside it, it does not.

This works the same for every key-level type — swings, PD/PW levels, and HTF or HTF² FVGs. The window is always counted in HTF candles, even for an HTF² level: the HTF candle is the unit.

  • Direction matters. A high-side break (swing high, PDH, PWH) or a bearish gap opens the window for bearish formations; a low-side break or a bullish gap opens it for bullish ones. Sweep into the level, reverse away from it.
  • Each level triggers once. A level that has already been swept or broken is spent — it does not open a window again.

Once a formation has earned the mark it keeps it for life — a C3 formation inherits it from its C2 phase, and a formation already alive when the contact happens picks it up too.

The part most people missThe filter only sees what you show it. It only counts a level type whose display toggle is on. Turn a toggle off and that level type drops out of the filter entirely, it stops marking formations. If HTF² FVG is off, no formation ever qualifies through an HTF² gap. Same for HTF FVG, HTF Swings, HTF² Swings, PDH/PDL and PWH/PWL, no exceptions. This is your main lever: switch off the levels you do not care about, leave on the ones you do.

Max vs. memory

Max limits drawing, not tracking. FVGs: the indicator remembers up to 3× your Max per direction, and the filter checks all of them. Swings: it keeps up to 30 open swings per side, every untouched one counts. So with FVG Max = 1 you can get a key-level formation from a gap that is not drawn anymore. The formation is real, you just cannot see its gap. Raise Max to see what triggered the mark.

ExceptionSMT formations always pass both filters. The divergence is their own confluence, they do not need a level.

SMT & PSPDivergence between correlated instruments.

Show SMT
Master switch for all SMT features (color, toggle, width). Off means all SMT off.
HTF SMT
Divergence at HTF swing points.
CTF SMT
Divergence at chart-timeframe swing points.
CTF SMT History
How many CTF divergences to keep on screen per side. 1 (default) shows only the latest, as before. Higher keeps older ones too — each stays until price takes its extreme, or it drops off past the limit.
PDH/PDL SMT
Divergence at the previous day high and low.
PWH/PWL SMT
Divergence at the previous week high and low.
SMT on HTF Candle
Draws divergence on the HTF candle boxes.
SMT Mode
The instruments you are compared against. Automatic recognizes your instrument and uses two correlated peers from the list below (e.g. NQ → ES + YM) — always both. Custom lets you set the peers yourself.
Enable Second SMT?
Applies in Custom only. Off compares you against a single peer (Peer 1) — a touch lighter, since the second symbol is no longer fetched. Automatic always uses both list peers, so this has no effect there. On is the default.
Peer 1 · Peer 2 · Inverse?
In Custom, type your two peer symbols. Each has an Inverse? checkbox for instruments that move opposite to yours (e.g. DXY), so the divergence still reads correctly.
PSP (Precision Swing Point)
Compares closing direction candle by candle. You close up, a peer closes down, that is a PSP. Color, toggle, style (Color or Dot), Max count. Require C2 only shows a PSP where there is also a C2 closure. Require SMT only where SMT divergence is present too. PSP on HTF Candle draws PSP markers on the HTF boxes.

Advanced · comparing four assets Custom compares you against two peers, so you plus two peers is three assets at once. To watch a fourth (say Gold, Silver, XAUEUR and XAUGBP together), add the indicator to the chart a second time:
  • First copy — SMT on Custom: Peer 1 SI1!, Peer 2 FOREXCOM:XAUEUR
  • Second copy — turn everything off except SMT, set Asset Label off, also Custom: Peer 1 FOREXCOM:XAUGBP, Peer 2 GC1!
Now you see SMTs across all four. The one trade-off: on the Silver chart, an SMT between Silver and Gold carries no asset label (same for the XAUGBP ones).

Then save it as an Indicator Template. On any Gold or Silver chart, load the template and every setting applies automatically. Keep a second template for standard use (SMT = Auto) so you can switch back in one click.

08

Alarm FilterFires a TradingView alert when a chosen event happens.

Pick your events with the checkboxes, then create one alert in TradingView using "Any alert() function call."

Key Level Filter
The alert side of section 5. On means the formation alerts (HTF Sweep, C2 Closure, C3 Closure, CISD, CISD Touch, Continuation CISD) only fire when the formation is at a key level. A sweep alert only fires if the sweep itself hits one. Level alerts (FVG Touch, Swing Breach, PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL) are untouched by this filter. Independent of "Draw only at Key Levels", so your chart can show everything while your phone only pings on the ones that matter.
Session Filter
Master toggle. Alerts only fire during enabled sessions.
Asia / London / NY AM / NY PM
Four sessions, each with a toggle and a time window. Times use the General-group timezone.
Event checkboxes
HTF Sweep · HTF C2 Closure · HTF C3 Closure · CISD · CISD Touch (price touches a pending CISD, get ready before confirmation) · Continuation CISD · PDH/PDL Break · PWH/PWL Break · HTF FVG Touch · CTF FVG Touch · HTF² FVG Touch · HTF Swing Breach · CTF Swing Breach · HTF² Swing Breach.

Setup: right-click the chart → Add Alert → condition = Levl Up → "Any alert() function call."

Good to knowTo alert on a candle closure, create the alert from a lower timeframe. For a 4h closure use 15m/30m, for a 30m closure use 3m. The HTF² alerts (FVG Touch, Swing Breach) only work while the matching HTF² display toggle is on, the indicator only tracks what it shows.
Set up first, create the alert lastTradingView freezes your indicator settings the moment you create an alert. If HTF² FVG was off when you created it, that alert will never track HTF² gaps, even if you turn the toggle on afterwards. Get every setting right, then create the alert. Changed something later? Delete the alert and make a new one.
Worked example

1H C2 closure, but only at a 4H key level

This ties together custom alignment (section 2), HTF² (sections 3 and 4) and the Key Level Filter (section 5). Alerting on a 1H closure means running the alert from a lower TF, so you build this on the 5m chart.

  1. TF Alignment → Custom → 1h / 4h. On the 5m chart your HTF is already 1H. This mapping makes HTF² resolve to 4H instead of the automatic Daily, so "one level above your HTF" is now the 4H.
  2. On the 5m chart, turn on HTF² Swings and HTF² FVG. You now see the 4H swing highs and lows and 4H gaps drawn on your 5m chart.
  3. To count only 4H levels, turn off everything else that feeds the filter: HTF FVG, HTF Swings, and PDH/PDL + PWH/PWL (those last two are on by default).
  4. In the Alarm Filter group, turn on Key Level Filter and check HTF C2 Closure.
  5. Create the alert on the 5m chart ("Any alert() function call").

Result: you get pinged on every 1H C2 closure that forms at a 4H FVG or 4H swing high/low. Nothing else.

Why not via CTF closures: a CTF closure cannot confirm whether the lower timeframe formed a CISD. A 1H C2 closure needs a 5m CISD to validate, and a CTF closure drawn on the 1H chart has no reliable way to see that 5m CISD. The higher-TF closure path above is the correct way to build this.
