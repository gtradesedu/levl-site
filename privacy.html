Last updated: 22 July 2026
The short version

This website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. We do not collect personal data from you here. When you click through to Whop, Discord, TradingView or Lucid Trading, you are on their platforms and their privacy policies apply.

1. Who is responsible

Responsible for the processing of personal data on this website within the meaning of applicable data protection law (Swiss FADP and, where relevant, the EU GDPR) is the site operator named in our Imprint.

Contact for any data protection matter: help@levlhq.com

2. What this website collects

Directly: nothing. There is no registration, no contact form, no newsletter signup, no shopping cart and no comment function on this site. We set no cookies and run no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Our typefaces and images are served from our own domain, so loading this site contacts no third party at all.

Server logs

Our hosting provider automatically processes technical data when you load a page, as every web server does. This typically includes your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, referrer, and browser and operating system information. This is necessary to deliver the site and to maintain security and stability. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). These logs are retained only for a short period by the provider and are not combined with other data or used to identify you.

Hosting provider: Cloudflare Pages.

When you buy a product, Whop provides us with the limited data we need to deliver it and support you: for example your Whop username, order and subscription status, and the TradingView username you supply so we can grant access to the indicator. 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may also withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at help@levlhq.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Switzerland the FDPIC (edoeb.admin.ch), in the EU your national data protection authority.

6. Data security and transfers

This site is served over HTTPS. Some of the providers listed above are based outside Switzerland and the EEA, including in the United States. Where data is transferred there, those providers rely on their own safeguards, such as EU standard contractual clauses. Please refer to their policies.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the site or our services change. The date at the top shows the current version.
