Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026
The short version

This website itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The Levl Cockpit at app.levlhq.com is different: it has accounts, it stores your trading journal, and it needs your email address to do that. Everything below says exactly what is stored, where, and how to get rid of it.

1. Who is responsible

Responsible for the processing of personal data on this website and in the Levl Cockpit within the meaning of applicable data protection law (Swiss FADP and, where relevant, the EU GDPR) is the site operator named in the imprint.

Contact for any data protection matter: help@levlhq.com

2. This website (levlhq.com)

No accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics. Our hosting provider processes standard server logs, including your IP address, to deliver the pages and defend against attacks. Some links to partners are affiliate links; whether you click them is not tracked by us.

3. The Levl Cockpit (app.levlhq.com)

The Cockpit is a trading journal. To sync it across your devices you create an account. This is what that means:

  • Account data: your email address, and a password hash if you set one. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google.
  • Journal content: everything you type in — trades, notes, screenshots, accounts, strategies. This is your data. We do not read it, analyse it, sell it or share it.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Without this data the service cannot work.
  • Retention: as long as your account exists. Delete it and the data goes with it, immediately and without a copy on our side. See section 6.

Everything you enter is also stored locally in your browser so the app stays fast. Clearing your browser storage removes that local copy; the cloud copy is unaffected.

4. Processors we use

We do not run our own servers. These providers process data on our behalf under data processing agreements:

  • Supabase — accounts, sign-in and the journal database. Project hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Supabase Inc. is US-based; transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, and storage for the screenshots you upload. Cloudflare Inc. is US-based; transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Resend — sends the sign-in, password reset and security emails. Receives your email address only.

One more provider sits next to the Cockpit rather than inside it, and only sees you if you go there yourself:

  • Whop — the sales platform for the paid Levl Up indicator. If you subscribe, your purchase and billing data are processed there under Whop's own privacy notice. The Cockpit does not send anything to Whop.

The JavaScript library the app needs is served from levlhq.com itself. No content delivery network sees your IP address while the Cockpit loads.

5. Age

The Cockpit is aimed at adults. Do not create an account if you are under 18. We do not knowingly process data of children; if you believe a minor has registered, write to help@levlhq.com and the account will be removed.

6. Optional features that send data elsewhere

The AI features only run when you start them, and none is on by default. The market data below is described separately because part of it loads on its own.

  • AI features. If you enter your own API key and use the journal chat, the setup recognition or the coach, the relevant journal text and, where applicable, chart screenshots are sent to the provider you selected (Google, OpenAI or Anthropic) for that request. On free API tiers some providers may use submitted content to improve their models. Do not use these features for anything you would not want a third party to see.
  • Economic calendar. High-impact events are loaded through my own Cloudflare worker, never from the original source directly, so no third party sees your IP address. This happens automatically when you open the Daily Outlook, so that the news field can fill itself, and again when you press the calendar button. No journal content is ever sent along. If the worker is unavailable you simply get no calendar.
  • News ticker. Opening the News page fetches headlines from financialjuice.com directly, so that site sees your IP address for that request. It loads only when you open that page. No journal content is ever sent along.

7. Your rights

You can obtain a copy of your data at any moment without asking us: Settings → Backup writes a single file containing everything, images included. It works without an account and without internet.

You can delete everything yourself: Settings → Delete account removes the cloud database entry, every stored image and the account itself. This cannot be undone.

Beyond that you have the rights to information, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority — in Switzerland the FDPIC, in the EU the authority of your place of residence. Write to help@levlhq.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the site or our services change. The date at the top shows the current version.
